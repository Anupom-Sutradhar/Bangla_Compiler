Bangla Programming Language - Backus-Naur Form (BNF)
====================================================

<program>       ::= <statement-list>
<statement-list>::= <statement> <statement-list> | ε

<statement>     ::= <var-decl>
                  | <assignment>
                  | <print-stmt>
                  | <if-stmt>
                  | <while-stmt>
                  | <block>

<var-decl>      ::= <type> <identifier> "=" <expression> ";"
<type>          ::= "সংখ্যা" | "দশমিক"
<assignment>    ::= <identifier> "=" <expression> ";"
<print-stmt>    ::= "দেখাও" "(" <expression> ")" ";"

<if-stmt>       ::= "যদি" "(" <expression> ")" <block> <else-part>
<else-part>     ::= "নাহলে" <block> | ε
<while-stmt>    ::= "যতক্ষণ" "(" <expression> ")" <block>
<block>         ::= "{" <statement-list> "}"

<expression>    ::= <equality>
<equality>      ::= <comparison> <equality-tail>
<equality-tail> ::= ("==" | "!=") <comparison> <equality-tail> | ε

<comparison>    ::= <term> <comparison-tail>
<comparison-tail> ::= (">" | ">=" | "<" | "<=") <term> <comparison-tail> | ε

<term>          ::= <factor> <term-tail>
<term-tail>     ::= ("+" | "-") <factor> <term-tail> | ε

<factor>        ::= <unary> <factor-tail>
<factor-tail>   ::= ("*" | "/" | "%") <unary> <factor-tail> | ε

<unary>         ::= ("+" | "-") <unary> | <primary>
<primary>       ::= <number> | <identifier> | "(" <expression> ")"

<number>        ::= integer or decimal literal using ASCII or Bangla digits
<identifier>    ::= Unicode letter or "_", followed by Unicode letters/digits/marks/"_"

Operator precedence, highest to lowest:
1. Parentheses
2. Unary +, -
3. *, /, %
4. +, -
5. >, >=, <, <=
6. ==, !=
